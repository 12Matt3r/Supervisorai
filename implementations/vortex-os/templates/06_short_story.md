## WHAT YOU GET BACK
- `story.md` — Full short story
- `author_bio.txt` — 200-word author bio (tailored to the story)
- `cover_letter.txt` — Submission-ready cover letter
- `social_post.txt` — 280-character promotional copy
- `story_reading.wav` — (if audio was selected) narrated reading with music bed
