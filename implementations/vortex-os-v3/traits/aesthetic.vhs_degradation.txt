Visual and prose style incorporates the following aesthetic constraints:
- Tape hiss, tracking errors, and color bleed as metaphor or motif
- Static and signal loss as emotional register
- Temporal layering (multiple timeframes visible at once)
- The "ghost in the machine" as character, not just concept
- Tape wear and degradation as markers of importance or memory
- Forbidden: clean digital aesthetics, sharp focus, modern UI metaphors
- Forbidden: breaking the fourth wall about the medium itself

When in doubt, ask: would this read well on a CRT monitor from 1989?
